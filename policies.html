WEATHER POLICY


THE SAFETY, COMFORT, AND OVERALL EXPERIENCE OF OUR GUESTS REMAIN THE HIGHTEST PRIORITIES AT PLATINUM TOURS & TRANSPORTATION.

• RESCHEDULE THEIR EXPERIENCE TO THE NEXT AVAILABLE DATE, SUBJECT TO AVAILABILITY; OR

• BE FULLY REFUNDED OR RECEIVE A BOOKING CREDIT VALID TOWARD A FUTURE PLATINUM TOURS EXPERIENCE

GUESTS ARE ENCOURAGED TO MONITER THE WEATHER FORECASTS BEFORE MAKING A RESERVATION. IF OFFICIAL WEATHER ADVISORIES OR HAZARDOUS CONDITIONS ARE FORECASTED PRIOR TO YOUR TOUR DATE, PLATINUM TOURS WILL PROACTIVELY COMMUNICATE ANY OPERATIONAL CHANGES. RESERVATIONS WILL NOT PROCEED WHEN CONDITIONS ARE DEEMED UNSAFE BY OUR MANAGEMENT TEAM.

BY CONFIRMING A RESERVATION, GUESTS ACKNOWLEDGE AND ACCEPT THAT OPERATIONAL DECISIONS REGARDING WEATHER ARE MADE SOLELY BY PLATINUM TOURS IN THE INTEREST OF GUEST SAFETY AND SERVICE EXCELLENCE.
